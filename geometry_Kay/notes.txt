what are neighbors? 
when you compute thermal accumulation you are asking "which previous laser paths are spatially close to the current path"
Those nearby paths are called neighbors 
Example: current vector midpoint (0.50, 0.20)
If another vector midpoint is (0.55, 0.18) and your thermal radius is 0.10 
then the distance is small enough that it counts as a neighbor. Path liekly still influences local temperature and heat may not have dissipated

In DED: heat diffuses spatially, nearby deposited regions affect new deposition. repeated exposure causes overheating, grain growth, distortion

KDTree is a fast sptial search. Find all points near this point 
why not just compare everything? suppose you have 200,000 vectors

    compare to every other vector
this is O(n^2) which is slow
